About this list

"Era" is when devotion took hold, not necessarily when the person lived. Dates for legendary figures are traditional. A figure honored across faiths carries every tradition that honors it, so it shows up under each one.

"Suppressed or de-listed" and "Popular first, canonized later" refer to the relevant religious or civil authority: usually the Catholic or Orthodox Church, sometimes a state.

Left out on purpose: the online "saints" culture that venerates mass shooters. It is documented by extremism researchers, but listing those names would serve its purpose.
